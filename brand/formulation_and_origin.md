# Boca Water — Formulation & Origin Research Brief

**Product Concept:** Ultra-high-end, **completely unflavored, crystal-clear sparkling water** packaged in a **transparent 12 fl oz (355 mL) PET can**, engineered for calm mental clarity with zero taste interference, zero cloudiness, and crisp carbonation.

---

## Executive Summary

Formulating a **functional, completely unflavored sparkling water** in a **transparent PET can** is one of the most unforgiving challenges in beverage science. Without citrus oils, botanicals, or sweeteners to hide bitterness—and without opaque aluminum to hide turbidity or UV degradation—every milligram of solute must pass four non-negotiable gates:

1. **Sub-threshold sensory neutrality** (zero bitterness, chalkiness, earthiness, or lingering aftertaste in plain carbonated water).
2. **Absolute optical clarity** (0.0 NTU visual appearance, zero polymerization haze, zero sedimentation over shelf life).
3. **Carbonation & pH stability** (stable at carbonic acid pH ~4.0–4.8, zero nucleation/gushing during canning).
4. **Regulatory cleanliness** (FDA GRAS or native mineral water compliance without triggering adulteration or unapproved drug-claim risks).

**Top Formulation Recommendation:** **Stack 1 ("The Purest Signal")** — **150 mg Suntheanine® L-Theanine** (well below its ~600–1,500 mg/L taste threshold in water, FDA GRN 209 cleared up to 250 mg/serving) + **natural monomeric silica (75 mg/L SiO₂, ~27 mg per can)** + a **precision low-sodium bicarbonate/magnesium mineral balance** (Mg²⁺ capped at 12–15 mg/L to deliver crispness without crossing the ~47 mg/L bitterness threshold). For an executive focus SKU, **Stack 2** adds **250 mg Cognizin® Citicoline** (clinically validated for attention/inhibition, neutral-clean solubility, requiring UV-blocked clear PET resin and cool storage).

**Top Origin Recommendation:** **Concept 1 (*Boca del Manantial* — Patagonian / Southern Andean Volcanic Basalt Spring Mouth)**, which unites the etymology of *Boca* (the geological "mouth" of a deep volcanic artesian spring), naturally high monomeric silica (~75 mg/L) for a silky-crisp palate, and a visual metaphor of volcanic rock filtration that makes the transparent PET can feel like a geological lens.

## Part 1: Flavorless Mental-Clarity Additives (Zero Taste, Zero Cloudiness, CO₂-Stable)

### 1.1 The Transparent-Can & Unflavored Engineering Constraint

Because a 12 fl oz can holds **355 mL (0.355 L)**, any added functional ingredient at dose $D$ (mg) reaches a concentration of $C = D / 0.355\text{ mg/L}$. In plain carbonated water (pH ~4.0–4.8), dissolved $\text{CO}_2$ sharpens trigeminal bite and amplifies metallic or bitter off-notes. Furthermore, **clear PET cans** introduce two packaging constraints that directly affect nootropics:
- **UV / High-Energy Visible (HEV) Light (290–400+ nm):** Standard clear PET transmits UV light, which catalyzes free-radical oxidation of amino acids and nucleotides when trace dissolved oxygen is present. Clear PET preforms **must** be compounded with an optically clear UV absorber (e.g., Milliken ClearShield® or equivalent) and filled at **<0.1 ppm dissolved oxygen (DO)**.
- **CO₂ Permeability:** Standard thin-walled clear PET cans typically hold acceptable carbonation (≥6.0–6.2 g/L $\text{CO}_2$) for **3–6 months** at ambient temperature; extending shelf life requires stretch-blow-molding (ISBM) barrier optimization, high-barrier PET coatings, and cool storage (<20°C / 68°F).

### 1.2 Candidate-by-Candidate Technical Evaluation

#### 1. L-Theanine (Suntheanine® — Pure Enzymatically Fermented L-Isomer)
* **(a) Clinical Mechanism & Effective Dose (per 12 fl oz):** Crosses the blood-brain barrier and increases **alpha brain wave activity (8–13 Hz)** within 30–60 minutes, producing "relaxed alertness" (calm focus without sedation) while attenuating acute stress markers (cortisol, heart-rate reactivity).
  * **Clinical range:** 50 mg (mild stress blunting), **100–200 mg** (robust EEG alpha-wave elevation in double-blind, placebo-controlled trials).
  * **Recommended Boca dose:** **100–150 mg per 12 fl oz can** (282–423 mg/L), or up to **200 mg** (563 mg/L) for maximum standalone effect.
* **(b) Sensory & Taste Impact in Plain Carbonated Water:** Pure L-theanine is virtually odorless with a faint sweet/umami character at high concentrations. Crucially, published sensory thresholds in water sit at **0.06% to 0.15% (600–1,500 mg/L, ~8.6 mM)**. At **100–150 mg per 355 mL (282–423 mg/L)**, Suntheanine sits **well below the human detection threshold**—imparting zero bitterness, zero aroma, and at most an imperceptible softening of carbonic bite.
* **(c) Optical Clarity & Solubility:** Highly water-soluble; yields a **100% clear, colorless solution** with zero haze or sediment at functional doses up to 250 mg/serving.
* **(d) Carbonation, pH & PET Stability:** Highly stable across acidic beverage ranges (**pH 3.0–6.0**) and standard canning/pasteurization. Because Boca Water contains **zero reducing sugars**, Maillard browning/yellowing is eliminated. Requires low-DO filling and UV-absorbing clear PET resin to prevent slow photo-oxidation on retail shelves.
* **(e) FDA GRAS / Beverage Status:** **Gold-standard regulatory clearance.** Suntheanine® received an FDA **"Letter of No Objection" under GRAS Notice GRN 209** (2007) specifically permitting up to **250 mg per serving** in conventional beverages, explicitly including **specialty bottled waters**.

---

#### 2. Citicoline / CDP-Choline (Cognizin® — Fermented Cytidine 5'-Diphosphocholine)
* **(a) Clinical Mechanism & Effective Dose (per 12 fl oz):** Hydrolyzes into cytidine and choline, crossing the blood-brain barrier to fuel **phosphatidylcholine synthesis** (neuronal membrane integrity via the Kennedy pathway), **acetylcholine production** (attention and working memory), and **frontal-lobe mitochondrial ATP output**.
  * **Clinical range:** **250 mg/day** significantly reduced omission and commission errors on the Continuous Performance Test II (CPT-II) in healthy adults (McGlade et al., 2012) and improved selective attention/impulse control (McGlade et al., 2019); 500 mg/day is used in longer-term memory trials (Nakazaki et al., 2021).
  * **Recommended Boca dose:** **250 mg per 12 fl oz can** (704 mg/L).
* **(b) Sensory & Taste Impact in Plain Carbonated Water:** Very clean, **neutral to faintly acidic** profile—dramatically cleaner than Alpha-GPC or B-vitamins. In carbonated water (which already carries mild carbonic acidity), 250 mg blends smoothly without bitterness, earthiness, or astringency; bench sensory validation is still advised at 250 mg vs. 125 mg in unflavored water to ensure zero subtle saline/acidic shift.
* **(c) Optical Clarity & Solubility:** Exceptionally water-soluble; maintains **crystal optical clarity** with zero turbidity when fully dissolved.
* **(d) Carbonation, pH & PET Stability:** Stable across acidic RTD environments (**pH 3.4+**) and thermal processing (even hot-fill ~95°C). **Important shelf-life caveat:** Over long ambient storage at elevated heat (>30°C / 86°F), citicoline can slowly hydrolyze into choline and cytidine monophosphate (CMP). Best paired with **cool-storage / cold-chain distribution (<20°C)** and UV-blocked clear PET.
* **(e) FDA GRAS / Beverage Status:** **Self-Affirmed GRAS** for conventional foods and functional beverages in the U.S.; fermented, non-GMO, allergen-free, Kosher.

#### 3. Natural Silica / Monomeric Orthosilicic Acid ($\text{Si(OH)}_4$, expressed as $\text{SiO}_2$)
* **(a) Clinical Mechanism & Effective Dose (per 12 fl oz):** Monomeric orthosilicic acid is the bioavailable form of silicon in natural volcanic waters. Long-term French epidemiological cohorts (**PAQUID** and **EPIDOS**) link higher daily silica water intake with **lower risk of cognitive decline**, acting via the **"aluminum antidote" mechanism**—binding neurotoxic $\text{Al}^{3+}$ into inert hydroxyaluminosilicates for renal excretion without depleting iron or copper.
  * **Effective / benchmark range:** **60–90 mg/L $\text{SiO}_2$** (~21–32 mg $\text{SiO}_2$ per 12 fl oz can), matching iconic volcanic waters like Antipodes (~73–78 mg/L) and FIJI (~93 mg/L).
* **(b) Sensory & Taste Impact in Plain Carbonated Water:** **100% tasteless and odorless.** Rather than adding flavor, dissolved silica modifies **mouthfeel**—creating a prized smooth, silky, velvety glide on the palate that elevates the crispness of fine carbonation.
* **(c) Optical Clarity & Solubility (Critical Haze Limit):** Monomeric orthosilicic acid remains stably dissolved and **100% optically clear up to ~100–120 mg/L $\text{SiO}_2$** at room temperature. **Never exceed 90 mg/L $\text{SiO}_2$** in a transparent can: above ~100–120 mg/L (or under cold-shock/alkaline shifts), OSA undergoes autopolycondensation into colloidal silica nanoparticles, causing **visible milky haze**.
* **(d) Carbonation & pH Stability:** Mildly acidic carbonated water (pH 4.0–5.0) **stabilizes** monomeric orthosilicic acid and inhibits polymerization far better than strongly alkaline water. **Sourcing note:** Naturally occurring volcanic-aquifer silica is far more stable (and less prone to precipitation) than externally spiked silicate additives.
* **(e) FDA GRAS / Beverage Status:** Naturally occurring silica in spring/artesian/mineral water is a **native mineral constituent** (zero food-additive or GRAS hurdle, ultra-clean label).

#### 4. Trace Ionic Magnesium (Bicarbonate / Buffered Bisglycinate vs. Chloride & L-Threonate)
* **(a) Clinical Mechanism & Effective Dose (per 12 fl oz):** Essential cofactor for NMDA receptor modulation, GABAergic calm, and neuronal ATP stabilization. However, **sensory limits dictate a micro-dose approach** in unflavored sparkling water:
  * **Unflavored sparkling ceiling:** **10–25 mg/L elemental $\text{Mg}^{2+}$** (~3.5–9 mg per 12 fl oz can). This is a **sub-therapeutic sensory/electrolyte micro-dose**—enough to contribute crispness and mineral balance, not a full 200 mg supplement dose.
* **(b) Sensory & Taste Impact (Form Matters Critically):**
  * **Magnesium Chloride ($\text{MgCl}_2$):** **Avoid above trace levels.** Notoriously bitter, briny, and astringent; consumer sensory rejection begins as low as **~47 mg/L**.
  * **Magnesium Bicarbonate (native aquifer or $\text{MgCO}_3$ + $\text{CO}_2$):** **Ideal.** Imparts bright, invigorating "crispness" and structural snap at 10–25 mg/L without bitterness when buffered by bicarbonate.
  * **Magnesium Bisglycinate (taste-optimized beverage grade):** Chelation masks metallic bitterness and raises the taste threshold, though high doses still add faint sweet/amino notes and risk slight haze if poorly chelated or interacting with trace ions.
  * **Magnesium L-Threonate (Magtein®):** Strong cognitive positioning, but carries a distinct characteristic taste note that is **detectable in unflavored water** and requires strict particulate filtration. **Not recommended** for 100% unflavored Boca Water.
* **(c) Optical Clarity & Solubility:** Native magnesium bicarbonate and high-purity $\text{MgCl}_2$ are 100% clear. Beverage-grade bisglycinate requires strict pH/ion compatibility testing to prevent slow precipitation over 6 months in clear PET.
* **(d) Carbonation & pH Stability:** Dissolved $\text{CO}_2$ keeps magnesium bicarbonate stably in solution (carbonic acid prevents carbonate scale precipitation).
* **(e) FDA GRAS / Beverage Status:** Magnesium salts have GRAS status, **but** intentionally fortifying a carbonated water triggers the FDA **Nutrient Addition Policy (21 CFR 104.20)**, which discourages indiscriminate fortification of carbonated beverages. Native aquifer magnesium (or standardized mineral remineralization) is the cleanest compliance path.

---

#### 5. Balanced Trace Mineral Electrolytes (Bicarbonate, Calcium, Potassium, & Trace Lithium)
* **(a) Role & Optimal Sparkling Water Matrix (mg/L):** Hydration itself is a primary driver of cognitive sharpness (even 1–2% dehydration impairs attention and executive speed). For an ultra-crisp, "nothing in between" sparkling water, water sommeliers and sensory panels identify an optimal structure:
  * **TDS:** **130–350 mg/L** (below ~100 mg/L tastes thin/sharp; above ~800 mg/L tastes heavy/salty).
  * **Bicarbonate ($\text{HCO}_3^-$):** **100–220 mg/L** — buffers carbonic acid sting into a smooth, dry finish.
  * **Calcium ($\text{Ca}^{2+}$):** **15–55 mg/L** — light structural body (stay well below the 100 mg/L chalkiness ceiling).
  * **Potassium ($\text{K}^+$):** **2–8 mg/L** — adds imperceptible roundness (exceeding 15–20 mg/L introduces brackish/medicinal off-notes).
  * **Sodium ($\text{Na}^+$):** **<15 mg/L** — keeps the finish crisp rather than club-soda salty.
* **(b) Critical Regulatory & Formulation Rule on Lithium:**
  * **Naturally occurring trace lithium** (e.g., 0.01–0.5 mg/L native to certain deep volcanic or lithia springs) is legally permitted in **bottled mineral water** as a conventional food (the FDA has no specific MCL for native lithium in bottled water), though it **cannot** carry psychiatric or mood-treatment drug claims.
  * **Deliberately added lithium salts** (e.g., lithium orotate, citrate, chloride) have **NO FDA-affirmed GRAS status** for beverages. Adding lithium to sparkling water renders the product **adulterated/misbranded** under the FFDCA and invites FDA Warning Letters. **Verdict:** Never add exogenous lithium; only leverage trace lithium if naturally native to a chosen spring source, and never make therapeutic claims.

---

#### 6. Paraxanthine (enfinity®) vs. Caffeine-Free Options
* **(a) Clinical Mechanism & Effective Dose:** Paraxanthine (1,7-dimethylxanthine) is caffeine's primary active metabolite—delivering clean adenosine-receptor antagonism, focus, and alertness with less jitteriness/crash than caffeine. Self-Affirmed GRAS supports **100–300 mg/serving**.
* **(b) Sensory Reality in Unflavored Water:** While enfinity® is **~22% less bitter than caffeine**, it is still a methylxanthine. In flavored RTDs, light citrus masks it easily; in **100% unflavored sparkling water**, 100–200 mg of paraxanthine introduces a **detectable dry bitterness** on the back palate that breaks the "pure water, nothing in between" illusion.
* **(c) Optical Clarity & Stability:** Water-soluble and clear at target pH, though long-term cold-storage recrystallization checks are required.
* **(d) Strategic Verdict:** **Keep Flagship Boca Water 100% Caffeine-Free & Xanthine-Free.** Calm, all-day mental clarity (drinkable at 9 AM or 8 PM, at a Michelin dining table or a boardroom) is a far stronger ultra-luxury positioning than stimulant water. Reserve paraxanthine only for a future lightly flavored/botanical SKU if ever expanded.

## Part 2: Water Source & Brand Origin Backstory ("Boca")

### 2.1 The Etymological & Sensory Power of "Boca"

In Spanish, Portuguese, and Italian, ***boca*** means **"mouth"**—carrying a rare double resonance for an ultra-luxury unflavored sparkling water:
1. **Hydrological / Geological Meaning:** *Boca* denotes the physical threshold where hidden earth meets open air—the **mouth of a spring** (*boca de manantial*), the **mouth of a glacial river**, the **vent of a volcanic formation** (*boca volcánica*), or a **coastal rock inlet** (*Boca de Ratones*).
2. **Sensory / Palate Meaning:** *Boca* is the mouth itself—the instrument of taste. For a crystal-clear, completely unflavored water in a transparent can, the brand promise becomes literal: **from the mouth of the earth to yours, with nothing in between.**
